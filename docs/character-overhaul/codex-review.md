# Independent character-overhaul QA review

Reviewed `d753237..c3e1020` on `character-overhaul`, 2026-10-24, as game art director and gameplay engineer. **Recommendation: hold release for grip/contact corrections and phone validation.** The painted outfits have stronger identity and detail, but the current rig does not consistently connect them to weapons or terrain.

Evidence: the five supplied images, source inspection, `npm test` (**71/71 pass**), `npm run typecheck` (**pass**), and an in-memory `tsx` solver probe. No files changed except this report; no commits or deployment. The existing working-tree edit to `tools/perf.mjs` was left untouched. Stills cannot establish motion quality or mobile frame time; performance and map-clearance concerns below are risks, not measured failures.

## 1. Visible art defects

- **High — silhouette/readability:** image 2's player merges into the brown trunk/crate/earth; fine olive/gold armour detail collapses at phone size, leaving the chevron and cyan lens as stronger identifiers than the body. Ninja's near-black limbs also merge together in images 3–4. Establish larger light/dark masses, broader team-colour areas and a restrained separation rim; inspect all four map themes at actual CSS size.
- **High — arms and joints:** images 3–4 show long guns projecting well beyond the support hand, overlapping forearms that obscure the chest, and circular armour shoulder/knee pieces that read as separate pasted discs. The far-hand disconnect is numerically confirmed below; joint overlap and painted edge cleanup need another pass after correcting reach.
- **Medium — accessory fit:** image 5's helmet, beret and cap perch above substantial uncompressed hair; helmet coverage leaves an implausibly exposed crown/forehead profile, especially on the ninja bun, while the cap rides high on the quiff. The visor is a large white slab covering much of the upper face; goggles/shades have thick projecting front edges. These are shared accessories positioned from head bounds rather than individual anatomical anchors (`heroLook.ts:19`, `:97`; pipeline `build_rig.py:394`, `:410`). Add per-outfit fits and hair occlusion, including bun handling.
- **Medium — scale/style integration:** image 1's new body is more elongated and visually thin than the old compact trooper, with a narrow waist and small lower legs relative to head/weapon; the suit and ninja are especially spindly in image 4. Detailed painted shading and fine outlines compete with the broad flat cartoon terrain, HUD and chunky flame/flash art in images 2–4. A unified outline/value pass matters more than adding detail.
- **Medium — lighting/part seams:** highlights remain baked into each cut-out while limbs rotate, and mirroring reverses their light direction; armour discs and boots can consequently read as differently lit surfaces rather than one body. This is an inherent cut-out limitation, not proof of a specific broken texture seam. No unequivocal open transparent joint gap is identifiable from these reduced sheets.
- **Low — preview presentation:** image 1's outfit thumbnails have substantial unused area, reducing the benefit of the high-resolution atlas; image 3 crops the top row and rightmost columns, and image 4 crops the rightmost death body, weakening their value as complete QA evidence.

## 2. Animation and renderer findings

- **High, confirmed — unreachable support grips.** `rig.ts:134` clamps IK reach to 20.45 units, but `:344` targets the weapon pivot plus the fore-grip; rifle target is roughly 34.4 units from the back shoulder. A settled horizontal-aim probe using `weaponFit(1..6)` measured hand-to-target gaps of **0.66, 5.19, 13.97, 9.28, 13.54 and 8.59 world units**, respectively. IK remains finite but silently misses the grip; move gun/grip targets into anatomical reach, rather than merely stretching the forearm. Existing hand coverage only checks the near arm (`test/hero.test.ts:71`).
- **High, confirmed — feet slide and landing penetrates.** The stance is a local cosine sweep (`rig.ts:211–227`), not a world-space planted foot: at 215 units/s and 2.2 cycles/s, its maximum backward foot speed is only about 94 units/s, so the stance foot still moves forward. Whole-root lean further rotates contacts (`HeroView.ts:496`). Landing adds crouch to both hip and foot-target height (`rig.ts:274`, `:301–302`), moving the ground target downward instead of keeping it fixed; after a 380-unit/s fall and six landing frames the probe found sole heights **40.82/40.08**, versus ground at shoulder +38. Terrain slopes are never supplied to the rig.
- **Medium, confirmed — flash duration depends on frame rate.** `HeroView.ts:564` subtracts a fixed 1/60 per update from the 0.055-second flash timer: roughly four frames last ~133 ms at 30 fps and ~33 ms at 120 fps. Use elapsed time.
- **Medium — stale animation after culling/respawn.** Offscreen returns precede death advancement and solver updates (`HeroView.ts:424–447`), so corpses pause and can resume their tumble on re-entry. Respawn resets the rig and primary kick but leaves `kick2`, `climb`, `punchT`, and `flashT` (`:770–782`), allowing residual recoil/jab/flash if death interrupted them. Clear all transient state and advance lifecycle timers independently of visibility.
- **Medium — incomplete transition/facing validation.** The cosine dead band prevents rapid flip chatter (`HeroView.ts:451–453`), and aim is converted into mirrored local space (`rig.ts:292–294`), but foot memory is not remapped when face changes, so ground contacts can jump across the root. `hurtDir` is multiplied by face at the caller (`HeroView.ts:492`) and then used directly for world-root lean (`rig.ts:200`): left-facing flinches can lean opposite the equivalent right-facing hit. Test both facings at vertical aim and during backpedal/landing.
- **Medium, unmeasured — phone performance.** `HeroView.ts:160–190` creates three Images for every ordinary slot, even without tint/accent content; approximately 65 scene objects per hero means ~1,300 objects for 20 players before the map/effects. `applyPose` allocates a three-element array per part each frame (`:543`); input/grip objects, accessory/trinket results, and solver IK/rotation/foot objects add GC churn (`:473–494`; `rig.ts:156`, `:168`, `:207`, `:219`). Allocate only required layers, reuse temporaries and profile 20 visible players on Safari/Android before accepting this budget. Shared atlases help batching but do not remove update/transform cost.
- **Coverage limitation:** Rig Lab supplies velocities without moving roots (`dev/RigLab.ts:119–123`), so its attractive run preview cannot reveal world-space foot sliding; randomized `RigState.t` (`rig.ts:101`) also makes fresh captures nondeterministic unless seeded.

## 3. Customisation correctness

Shared `resolveLook`, `slotFrame`, `accessoryPos`, weapon metadata and `solveRig` substantially reduce menu/game drift (`sprites.ts:245–286`; `HeroView.ts:251–268`, `:473–497`). Both renderers choose skin variants first and composite player/accent overlays; no different outfit-index mapping was found. Existing saved indices remain meaningful, and missing/invalid values are sanitized (`App.ts:91`; `shared/src/look.ts:39–47`); this redesign does not require a saved-look migration.

- **Medium, confirmed:** team colour refresh is keyed only on `s.color` (`HeroView.ts:395`, `:287–292`), although colour resolves from both team and colour; a late roster/team update with unchanged personal colour leaves body/shield/name tint stale while relation markers update. Cache the resolved colour or include team in invalidation.
- **Medium, confirmed:** no-look menu fallback passes a *skin* index as the *colour* argument to `resolveLook` (`sprites.ts:247`), which then applies `defaultSkin(color)` (`heroLook.ts:47`); this differs from explicit `o.skin` semantics and gameplay's actual colour fallback. Normal sanitized profiles with explicit looks avoid this path.
- **Medium — colour fidelity:** a twin booster explicitly disables its player-tint layer (`HeroView.ts:260`), and menu code likewise draws only base/accent (`sprites.ts:327–328`), so it does not inherit the primary pack's team colour. Skin masking uses hue/value thresholds plus morphology (`build_rig.py:165–201`), which can include warm hair/beard/leather or leave dark skin shadows unchanged; inspect all four tones at full resolution before claiming clean skin separation.
- **Low — delivery consistency risk:** menu loads unversioned `herohi` URLs (`sprites.ts:48`, `:94`), whereas game atlas loading uses manifest URLs (`BootScene.ts:88–90`); stale cache/CDN variants can diverge despite shared rig code. No cache failure was reproduced here.

## 4. Gameplay/network risks

- **High, confirmed rollback mismatch:** classic rendering still anchors weapons at feet −29 (`SoldierView.ts:586`), while authoritative shots, prediction and aim guide now use feet −38 (`world.ts:148`, `:174`; `NetGame.ts:238`; `AimGuide.ts:5`). `?classic=1` is only an art fallback, not a gameplay-compatible rollback: its visible gun sits nine units below the shot origin and its short body understates the new hitbox.
- **High, requires gameplay validation:** height 44→52 is an 18% taller body; the new head interval moves from roughly feet −44..−31 to −52..−40 (`constants.ts:40–47`; `world.ts:587–589`). Ceiling collision already consumes `PLAYER.h` (`movement.ts:230–254`), so passage clearance, spawn safety, jump routes and bot navigation need all-map regression checks. Shoulder +9 changes cover shooting, projectile emergence and line of sight; these are balance changes, not cosmetic-only changes.
- **Medium:** posing can place head/limbs outside the fixed upright hit rectangles, particularly dash/hover/lean, with no matching hitbox deformation (`HeroView.ts:496`; `world.ts:587–589`). Draw hitbox overlays during aimed fire, both facings and slopes to assess unfair-looking misses/hits.
- **Medium:** the unscoped phone view changes 560→400 world units, reducing vertical coverage by ~29% (`GameScene.ts:32–37`); readability improves but offscreen attacks and platform awareness need phone playtesting. Desktop/tablet retain different sight coverage.
- **Protocol handling is sound at handshake:** v5 is sent by `Session.ts:72` and older versions are rejected with refresh notice/close 4005 (`room.ts:151–154`). Coordinate client/PWA and LAN-host updates; old cached clients cannot join a v5 host. Tests passing do not establish update recovery or 20-player network/mobile performance.
